# Project 1: Build Your Personal Website with AI!
In this project, you will leverage AI and your knowledge of HTML, CSS, and now, JavaScript to build a simple, single-page personal website that will showcase your projects.

You will use AI to help you brainstorm design ideas, apply web development best practices, and troubleshoot issues, bringing your vision to life on screen! 

We are also going to work on adding simple animation like buttons shifting on hover & even a little pixel sprite to keep users company on your page. Have fun and be creative!

Launch a Codespace to get started. **Remember to Commit and Push** your project changes to GitHub from Codespaces to prevent losing progress.
